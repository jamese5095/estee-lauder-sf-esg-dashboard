Carbon intensity explained

Volume sets the scale. Structure sets the intensity.

Carbon intensity measures emissions per shipped piece. A busier month does not automatically have a higher intensity; intensity rises only when emissions grow faster than shipment volume.

Volume alone is not the cause.

May’s emissions increased much faster than its shipment volume. January also handled more pieces than April or July, yet had the lowest emissions per piece.

>99.99%of both January and May volume was SF Standard Express, so the service-tier mix is unlikely to explain the change.
