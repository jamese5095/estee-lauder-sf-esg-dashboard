Notes

Reference definitions.

Open a term when a definition is needed; the core methodology stays concise.

Scope 3 Emission

Indirect value-chain emissions across upstream and downstream activities; represents 80–90% of a company’s overall carbon footprint.

SBTi · Science Based Targets initiative

Global benchmark coalition enabling businesses to set auditable, science-based emission reduction targets aligned with the 1.5°C Paris Agreement.

CSRD · Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive

Strict EU regulation requiring global enterprises to provide transparent, audited and standardized Scope 3 ESG disclosures.

GHG · Greenhouse Gas Protocol

The world’s most widely used greenhouse gas accounting standard, defining rules for Scope 1, 2 and 3 footprint assessment.

ISO 14083

The definitive international standard for quantifying and reporting greenhouse gas emissions across end-to-end logistics operations.

GLEC · Global Logistics Emissions Council

Universal accounting methodology for measuring logistics emissions across all freight modes.

UK DEFRA · Environment, Food & Rural Affairs

Official greenhouse gas conversion factor database used internationally to convert transport activity data into tCO₂e.
