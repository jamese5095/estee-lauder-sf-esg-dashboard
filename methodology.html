Calculation framework

Four relationships anchor the dashboard.

The current dashboard uses the SF Express net actual footprint. Opportunity estimates remain gated until the required operational fields and comparable emission factors are available.

Actual emissionsActivity × Emission factor

Activity may be tonne-km, vehicle-km, electricity, material mass or another method-specific driver.

Carbon intensityActual emissions ÷ Shipment volume

SF Jan–Jul weighted intensity is 279.01 gCO₂e/pcs. CN intensity is unavailable, not zero.

Estimated reductionEligible activity × (Current EF − Target EF)

The target scenario also requires adoption, operating feasibility, cost and service-level validation.

Optimized footprintCurrent SF actual − Estimated reduction

The result remains a scenario until activity, factors and operational constraints are validated.

Evidence classification

Three labels prevent false precision.

The interface keeps source facts, derived relationships and prospective scenarios visually and verbally distinct.

Actual / Reported

A value directly displayed in the 2026 source dashboard or extracted from its monthly records.

Calculated / Derived

A transparent arithmetic relationship, such as weighted intensity, calculated from reported values.

Scenario / Modeled

A prospective result that only becomes available after method-specific inputs and constraints are supplied.
