# Estée Lauder 2026 Monthly Emission Data

## Scope and source

- Source dashboard: `overall-and-product0901.html`
- Source repository: https://github.com/jamese5095/fenghe-carbon-platform
- Source commit: `ff3a3c5`
- Dashboard year: `2026`
- Data are transcribed from the dashboard's embedded `window.DATA` object.
- `Last Year Total` and `YOY` are retained because they are displayed series in the `Emission, absolute` chart; every row is indexed to its corresponding 2026 month.

## Data coverage and quality notes

- Express, SF; Logistics, CN; and Packaging contain January–July 2026 data.
- WHS, local contains January–June 2026 data only.
- 3PLs, local has no monthly `Emission, absolute` or `Emission, intensity` rows in the dashboard source.
- Logistics, CN `Emission, intensity` values are present but all are zero.
- Packaging has no monthly `Emission, intensity` table in the dashboard source.
- The WHS card and field names indicate `gCO2e/kg` and `kg`; the chart renderer displays a generic `gCO2e/pcs` and `K pcs` label. This document uses the WHS card and data-field semantics.
- Values are shown to two decimal places, matching the dashboard's displayed precision.
